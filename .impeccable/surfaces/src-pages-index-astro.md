# Home — surface brief

Scope: the home page (src/pages/index.astro) plus the shared shell (src/layouts/Base.astro). Visitor mode: Persuade, story-first — the page is an ambassador piece; giving is present but secondary.

Audience & job: US supporters (Ohio church and sewing communities) meeting the ministry for the first time or returning. They should leave knowing who this is, where the work happens (Hands of Grace sewing center, Libreville, Gabon), and what the work is — and feel it enough to click through to Give or About.

Action & proof: primary in-page action "Read our story"; giving routes to /give (processing links out to togetherwithgrace.org/donate). Proof is real only: founder trip counts, the four cost designations, 501(c)(3)/EIN, named people. No testimonials or impact stats exist — never fabricate them.

Chosen direction: Wax-Print Cloth (seed ec8d0899, user-locked pick; code-led). The page is one length of printed yardage: orange/navy wax-print fields (SVG repeats + crackle), selvage strips as wayfinding, muslin backing for reading, stitch/tag/pinked-edge component grammar. Memorable moment: the pattern parts — a pinked-edge window opens onto a real photograph of the sewing room (the site's single authored motion).

Constraints: Astro static on GitHub Pages; BASE_PATH-aware URLs; brand orange #E9670D / navy #002B3C binding; logo + real photos pending from the user (marked placeholder slots at real dimensions until then).
